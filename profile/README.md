# Climate Intelligence CoP

Welcome to the **Climate Intelligence Community of Practice (CoP)**.

This community brings together NORCAP deployees and partners working at the intersection of climate information systems, Impact-Based Forecasting (IBF), artificial intelligence, geospatial analytics, advanced data infrastructure and humanitarian decision support.

The CoP provides a shared space for collaboration, technical exchange and practical knowledge sharing. Its focus is to help transform weather, climate, hazard, exposure, vulnerability and impact information into accessible, timely and actionable climate intelligence products and services.

## Purpose

The Climate Intelligence CoP connects technical experts with operational users so that climate intelligence tools, workflows and services respond to real decision-making needs.

The community supports work related to early warning, anticipatory action, humanitarian response, disaster risk management and climate-resilient development.

## Objectives

- Strengthen collaboration and knowledge exchange among members working on climate information systems, AI, advanced analytics and IBF.
- Compare methodologies, identify common practices and support harmonization across tools, workflows and protocols.
- Build technical and operational capacity through demonstrations, peer learning, hands-on clinics and reusable learning materials.
- Promote responsible use of AI, machine learning, automation, large language models and AI agents in climate services.
- Improve interpretation and communication of forecast uncertainty, risks and potential impacts.
- Support user-centered design, testing and scaling of climate intelligence and decision-support tools.

## Areas Of Collaboration

- Climate information systems and interoperable data infrastructure
- Artificial intelligence, machine learning and advanced analytics
- Impact-Based Forecasting and anticipatory action
- Geospatial analytics and Earth observation
- Forecast uncertainty, verification and risk communication
- User-centered climate services and decision-support tools

## What This GitHub Organization Is For

This GitHub organization is intended to serve as the CoP's shared technical and knowledge repository. It can be used to host:

- Activity inventories and collaboration matrices
- Methodologies, workflows, triggers and protocols
- Technical notes, case studies and good-practice documents
- Training materials, tutorials, notebooks and webinar resources
- Prototype tools, scripts, dashboards and reusable templates
- GitHub Pages sites, online galleries and documentation hubs
- Issues for tracking community actions, feature requests and technical work

## Suggested Repository Structure

The CoP may organize its work through repositories such as:

- `knowledge-repository` - presentations, methodologies, documentation, training resources and webinar materials
- `activity-inventory` - member expertise, tools, ongoing initiatives and collaboration opportunities
- `ibf-methodologies` - IBF workflows, thresholds, triggers, risk matrices and operational protocols
- `tools-and-platforms` - reusable scripts, notebooks, prototypes and platform documentation
- `learning-resources` - tutorials, technical clinics, Jupyter notebooks, articles and guidance materials
- `field-use-cases` - operational examples, lessons learned, case studies and good-practice notes
- `community-operations` - meeting notes, action plans, calendars, newsletters and coordination documents

## Current Technical Themes

Members are currently working across a range of systems and initiatives, including:

- Climate information platforms for National Meteorological and Hydrological Services, Regional Climate Centers and Regional Specialized Meteorological Centers
- CAP-based alerting and dissemination workflows
- Automated data loading, WIS2Box and observation data transmission
- Geospatial infrastructure for raster data intake, computation, mapping and evaluation
- Continental multi-hazard systems and data infrastructure
- Analysis-ready cloud-optimized datasets
- Continuous risk monitoring and assessment
- AI/ML applications for forecasting, crop yield prediction, post-processing, downscaling, bias correction, risk analysis and automation
- Training, capacity building and technical clinics

- Opening issues for proposed activities, gaps, questions or feature requests

- Reviewing methodologies and helping harmonize approaches across initiatives

## Working Arrangements

The CoP coordination team convenes meetings, maintains the workplan, follows up agreed actions, facilitates collaboration and prepares periodic progress summaries.

Specific activities may have designated activity leads responsible for defining scope, coordinating participating members, tracking progress and documenting results.

## About NORCAP

NORCAP is a global provider of expertise to the humanitarian, development and peacebuilding sectors. The Climate Intelligence CoP brings together deployees and collaborators exploring how climate information, technology and operational expertise can support better decisions before, during and after crises.
